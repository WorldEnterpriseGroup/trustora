About Trustora

Employment infrastructure for intelligent work.

Trustora combines Employer of Record services with access to specialist people in AI, ML, quantum, physics, sciences, and shipped software.

Strong relationships across the European Union, Australia, and the United States.
Our role

We hold the operating details so expert people can keep doing the work.

Global hiring asks a company to connect ambition with local reality. Our role is to coordinate the employment layer around that connection: the documents, payroll, benefits, timing, questions, and handoffs that let a company employ specialist people without asking them to carry the complexity alone.

We are not trying to make employment invisible. We are trying to make it legible enough to operate with confidence.

How we work

The promise is a behavior.

These principles are deliberately practical. They are what integrity looks like when the question is a payroll correction, a country exception, or a first day that needs more care.

01

Clear before clever

We make the employment model, responsibilities, assumptions, and handoffs visible before work begins.

02

Local where it matters

Global hiring is a local practice. We surface country-specific questions instead of hiding them in fine print.

03

Human in the loop

A platform can organize work. People still need a reliable answer when a pay, leave, or documentation question arrives.

We would rather be useful than impressive.

That means asking for the country before promising the timeline. It means separating administration from advice. It means giving a team a responsibility map instead of a collection of confident nouns.

If an answer depends on local law, tax, immigration, or a specific working relationship, we say so and help coordinate the next right conversation.

Work with us

Bring the question behind the question.

Tell us what you are trying to build, where the person will work, and what feels unclear. We will start there.
