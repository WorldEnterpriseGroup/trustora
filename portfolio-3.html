Dubai Market Expansion - Business Growth

European luxury brand achieved 250% revenue growth in MENAP region through Trustora's strategic market entry consulting.

A leading European luxury fashion brand sought to expand into the lucrative Dubai and broader MENAP markets but faced cultural, regulatory, and operational challenges. Trustora provided end-to-end market entry consulting, including market research, regulatory compliance, partner identification, and cultural adaptation strategies. We established their regional headquarters in Dubai, built a network of high-net-worth distributors, and crafted a localized marketing strategy that resonated with Middle Eastern luxury consumers. Our team managed everything from legal entity formation to recruiting local talent through our EOR services. Within 24 months, the brand achieved 250% revenue growth in the region and established flagship stores in Dubai, Riyadh, and Doha.
