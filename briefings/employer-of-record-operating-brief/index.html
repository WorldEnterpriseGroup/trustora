The Employer of Record Operating Brief

A practical decision instrument for leaders deciding whether an EoR is the right employment layer for the next specialist hire.

Core proposition

An EoR is most useful when it creates a deliberate bridge between a real hiring need and a future operating model—not when it is sold as a shortcut around responsibility.

Designed for

Founders, people leaders, operators, and technical executives

A working instrument, not a trend summary.

The full brief includes the reasoning, questions, and practical prompts needed to use the material with a leadership or hiring team.

Working questions

Open the decision before the paperwork.

Bring these prompts into the next conversation. They are designed to expose assumptions, not create false certainty.

01

The decision is not only “can we hire?”

The useful question is whether the company can create a compliant, understandable, and sustainable employment relationship around the role it actually needs.

  • What work needs to happen?
  • Where will the person work?
  • Which responsibilities must remain with the client company?
02

The bridge has boundaries

An EoR coordinates a local employment relationship. It does not remove the client’s responsibility for direction, management, product decisions, or the quality of the work.

  • Which advice must come from a qualified local professional?
  • Where will the employee ask for help?
  • What should be documented before the first day?
