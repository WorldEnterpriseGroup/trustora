## Role level

Mid-level.

## Opportunity

This is a paid Trustora opportunity. Compensation and other employment terms are discussed with shortlisted candidates and depend on the scope of the role, location, and applicable requirements.

## Position Overview

The Contract & Strategic Initiatives Manager helps Trustora make complicated work legible and actionable. The role sits between contract and proposal context, internal delivery, client questions, and the records that let a distributed team make good decisions without losing the thread.

You will work across strategic initiatives that touch specialist talent, Employer of Record operations, employee intelligence, and partner or client commitments. The work is practical: clarify what has been agreed, identify what must happen next, surface risk early, and help the right owner move the work forward.

## Responsibilities

- Translate contract, statement-of-work, grant, or initiative language into obligation registers, milestones, decision points, and accountable owners.
- Coordinate cross-functional inputs from leadership, people operations, finance, legal, delivery, and technical or scientific subject-matter experts.
- Prepare concise briefs, decision memos, meeting records, action trackers, status notes, and supporting materials for internal and external conversations.
- Maintain a reliable source of truth for commitments, dependencies, approvals, risks, and changes across active initiatives.
- Support proposal, partnership, and capture work by organizing requirements, evidence, contributor inputs, review cycles, and submission-ready materials.
- Help identify delivery, scope, information-handling, and country-context questions that should be escalated to a qualified owner or adviser.
- Improve the templates and working practices that make Trustora’s strategic initiatives easier to review, hand over, and learn from.

## Required Qualifications

- Several years of experience in contract operations, program management, proposal development, procurement, grants, consulting, or a closely related operating role.
- Strong written communication and the ability to make dense material understandable without removing important caveats.
- Experience coordinating work across people with different disciplines, priorities, and levels of context.
- Sound judgment about confidential information, version control, approvals, and when a question needs specialist review.
- Comfort working with structured documents, spreadsheets, task systems, and collaboration tools in a remote environment.
- A practical interest in the employment, people, technical, or scientific context around the work—not only the administrative record.

## Working Standards

Trustora expects clear ownership, dependable follow-through, and records that another person can use. You should be able to work independently, ask precise questions, communicate a changed assumption early, and keep momentum without turning uncertainty into a hidden risk.

## Expected Outcomes

In this role, stakeholders can see what Trustora has committed to, what is changing, who owns the next decision, and which questions still require review. Initiatives move with fewer avoidable handoff failures, and the documentation created along the way remains useful after a meeting or proposal deadline has passed.
