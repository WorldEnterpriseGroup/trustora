## Position Overview

The GovCon Policy & Administration Fellowship / Internship – Strategic Initiatives is a professional development role designed for individuals who are interested in government contracting, grants, proposal development, strategic partnerships, policy administration, and business development. The Fellow/Intern will support Trustora's efforts to research, prepare for, and pursue government funding and contracting opportunities while developing practical experience in research, proposal coordination, and strategic administration.

This position is not limited to administrative work. Instead, the Fellow/Intern will actively contribute to strategic initiatives by researching funding opportunities, assisting with capture planning, coordinating proposal inputs, and building relationships with organizations, universities, research institutions, and industry partners. A major responsibility of this role is communicating with external organizations to explore whether future teaming or collaboration could make sense and working with Principal Investigators (PIs) and Key Personnel (KPs) to coordinate requests for Letters of Commitment or other supporting materials when leadership decides to pursue an opportunity.

The Fellow/Intern will support internal working materials such as ORBITAL Snapshots, Probability of Win (pWin) analyses, Return on Investment (ROI) assessments, proposal narratives, budget narratives, commercialization and sustainability notes, security and Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL) planning materials, and other documents that may support a grant or contract pursuit. The role requires collaboration with executive leadership, technical teams, researchers, finance, legal, and external partners to organize accurate drafts, cite available evidence, and surface open questions. Responsible leadership and qualified advisers retain responsibility for final pursuit decisions, representations of capability, and any legal, contractual, or solicitation requirements.

As a fully remote position, the Fellow/Intern is expected to maintain excellent communication, demonstrate professionalism, manage multiple priorities, and consistently deliver high-quality work while following the organization's Agile methodology, cybersecurity policies, and documentation standards.

## Responsibilities

The Fellow/Intern will support opportunity research and pursuit planning by identifying funding opportunities from federal, state, local, defense, research, and commercial agencies and helping leadership evaluate which opportunities may align with the organization's strategic goals. They will research funding agencies, review solicitation documents, summarize requirements, and prepare reports that support capture planning and proposal decisions.

A significant portion of this role involves communicating with external organizations to explore strategic partnerships and whether a Teaming Agreement or another collaboration should be considered for a future opportunity. The Fellow/Intern will develop professional relationships with industry partners, universities, nonprofit organizations, and research institutions while representing the organization with professionalism and integrity.

The Fellow/Intern will regularly communicate with Principal Investigators (PIs), Key Personnel (KPs), Subject Matter Experts (SMEs), consultants, and organizational partners to coordinate proposal activities when a pursuit requires their input. This includes requesting Letters of Commitment, collecting supporting documentation, coordinating proposal contributions, scheduling meetings, and tracking outstanding tasks against working deadlines.

The role includes supporting the preparation of ORBITAL Snapshots by collecting technical information, organizing research findings, summarizing project objectives, describing possible outcomes, documenting available organizational capabilities, and preparing executive-level summaries for internal review or conversations with potential sponsors and partners.

The Fellow/Intern will assist with proposal preparation by drafting technical narratives, management plans, project schedules, work breakdown structures, milestone plans, budget narratives, commercialization strategies, sustainability plans, resource allocation plans, security plans, Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL) frameworks, and other supporting materials. They will organize source information and flag missing evidence or requirements; responsible leaders and advisers will review the final content and any compliance determination.

The Fellow/Intern will participate in developing Probability of Win (pWin) analyses and Return on Investment (ROI) assessments by researching competitors, evaluating customer needs, analyzing documented organizational strengths, identifying proposal risks, and supporting leadership in making informed pursuit decisions. These analyses are decision-support materials, not forecasts or guarantees of an award.

The role also includes maintaining organized opportunity and proposal documentation, templates, version control, meeting notes, knowledge repositories, and internal process documentation to support consistency across multiple pursuits. The Fellow/Intern will identify opportunities to improve research, preparation, coordination, and organizational learning through lessons learned and process improvements.

## Required Qualifications

The successful candidate demonstrates excellent written and verbal communication skills and is capable of producing professional, accurate, and well-structured documentation suitable for government agencies, research institutions, industry partners, and executive leadership. They possess strong attention to detail, critical thinking, organizational skills, and the ability to manage multiple assignments while maintaining a high standard of quality.

The Fellow/Intern must be comfortable working in a fully remote environment and demonstrate professionalism, accountability, initiative, adaptability, and a willingness to continuously learn new concepts related to government contracting, grants, proposal development, artificial intelligence, and business strategy.

The candidate should have practical experience using AI harnesses such as Claude Code or equivalent AI-powered coding and research environments to improve research, document development, proposal writing, workflow automation, and technical analysis. All AI-generated content must be reviewed, verified, and refined before being used in official organizational documents.

The Fellow/Intern should possess working knowledge of modern Artificial Intelligence technologies and demonstrate literacy in concepts including BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers), YOLO (You Only Look Once), Large Language Models (LLMs), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Prompt Engineering. The individual should understand how these technologies support research, proposal development, automation, and strategic decision-making.

The successful candidate demonstrates strong document writing abilities and can prepare reports, summaries, meeting notes, research papers, proposal sections, executive briefings, and other professional documentation while following organizational writing standards.

## Working Standards

The Fellow/Intern is expected to follow the organization's Agile culture and actively participate in sprint planning, daily stand-up meetings, backlog refinement, sprint reviews, retrospectives, and continuous improvement activities. All assigned work should be planned, tracked, documented, and completed according to Agile principles while maintaining transparency with leadership and team members.

The individual must comply with all organizational cybersecurity policies and best practices when handling confidential information, proposal documentation, customer information, research data, organizational knowledge, and AI-generated content. This includes protecting sensitive information, using approved software and systems, maintaining secure credentials, following access control procedures, and ensuring that all project materials remain confidential.

The Fellow/Intern is expected to maintain organized documentation, consistent file structures, version control practices, and detailed records of meetings, communications, proposal activities, and strategic initiatives to support organizational knowledge management and long-term operational success.

The role requires a commitment to continuous learning, professional development, collaboration, ethical decision-making, and maintaining the highest standards of integrity while representing the organization internally and externally.

## Core Skills

The ideal candidate demonstrates strong capabilities in government contracting research, grant proposal development, technical writing, strategic planning, business analysis, project coordination, stakeholder communication, relationship management, documentation, and organizational planning.

The Fellow/Intern should be proficient in using AI harnesses to support research, document generation, coding assistance, workflow automation, and strategic analysis while understanding the importance of validating AI-generated content for accuracy, compliance, and quality.

The successful candidate possesses excellent analytical thinking, problem-solving, time management, communication, collaboration, leadership, adaptability, and organizational skills. They are capable of working independently in a remote environment while effectively collaborating with cross-functional teams, external partners, Principal Investigators, Key Personnel, and executive leadership to achieve organizational objectives.

## Expected Outcomes

By successfully performing this role, the Fellow/Intern will contribute to a clearer pipeline of researched opportunities, organized partner conversations, coordinated supporting inputs, draft ORBITAL Snapshots, and proposal working materials. The role may help leadership prepare and pursue grants and contracts and improve internal knowledge and proposal processes. It does not guarantee funding, an award, a Teaming Agreement, a Letter of Commitment, a proposal submission, or a particular employment outcome. The Fellow/Intern will also contribute to continuous process improvement, organizational knowledge management, and the responsible use of AI technologies to increase efficiency while maintaining review against applicable organizational and opportunity requirements.
