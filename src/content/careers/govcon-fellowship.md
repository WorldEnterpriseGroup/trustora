## Position Overview

The GovCon Policy & Administration Fellowship / Internship – Strategic Initiatives is a professional development role designed for individuals who are interested in government contracting, grants, proposal development, strategic partnerships, policy administration, and business development. The Fellow/Intern will play an important role in supporting the organization's efforts to identify, pursue, and secure government funding opportunities while developing practical experience in the complete government contracting lifecycle.

This position is not limited to administrative work. Instead, the Fellow/Intern will actively contribute to strategic initiatives by researching funding opportunities, assisting with capture planning, coordinating proposal development activities, and building relationships with organizations, universities, research institutions, and industry partners. A major responsibility of this role is communicating with external organizations to determine whether they are interested in establishing Teaming Agreements for future government opportunities and working closely with Principal Investigators (PIs) and Key Personnel (KPs) to obtain Letters of Commitment that strengthen government proposal submissions.

The Fellow/Intern will support the development of ORBITAL Snapshots, Probability of Win (pWin) analyses, Return on Investment (ROI) assessments, proposal documentation, budget narratives, commercialization strategies, sustainability plans, security plans, Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL) plans, and other supporting documents required for government grant and contract submissions. The role requires collaboration with executive leadership, technical teams, researchers, finance, legal, and external partners to ensure that every proposal is accurate, compliant, competitive, and aligned with organizational goals.

As a fully remote position, the Fellow/Intern is expected to maintain excellent communication, demonstrate professionalism, manage multiple priorities, and consistently deliver high-quality work while following the organization's Agile methodology, cybersecurity policies, and documentation standards.

## Responsibilities

The Fellow/Intern will participate in the complete government contracting and grant development process by identifying funding opportunities from federal, state, local, defense, research, and commercial agencies and assisting leadership in evaluating which opportunities align with the organization's strategic goals. They will conduct research on funding agencies, review solicitation documents, summarize requirements, and prepare reports that support capture planning and proposal decision-making.

A significant portion of this role involves communicating with external organizations to explore strategic partnerships and determine whether Teaming Agreements should be established for future government opportunities. The Fellow/Intern will develop professional relationships with industry partners, universities, nonprofit organizations, and research institutions while representing the organization with professionalism and integrity.

The Fellow/Intern will regularly communicate with Principal Investigators (PIs), Key Personnel (KPs), Subject Matter Experts (SMEs), consultants, and organizational partners to coordinate proposal activities. This includes requesting Letters of Commitment, collecting supporting documentation, coordinating proposal contributions, scheduling meetings, tracking outstanding tasks, and ensuring that required materials are submitted before proposal deadlines.

The role includes supporting the preparation of ORBITAL Snapshots by collecting technical information, organizing research findings, summarizing project objectives, identifying expected outcomes, documenting organizational capabilities, and preparing executive-level summaries that clearly communicate project value to potential sponsors and partners.

The Fellow/Intern will assist with proposal development by preparing technical narratives, management plans, project schedules, work breakdown structures, milestone plans, budget narratives, commercialization strategies, sustainability plans, resource allocation plans, security plans, Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL) frameworks, and other proposal documentation required for successful government submissions.

The Fellow/Intern will participate in developing Probability of Win (pWin) analyses and Return on Investment (ROI) assessments by researching competitors, evaluating customer needs, analyzing organizational strengths, identifying proposal risks, and supporting leadership in making informed bid decisions.

The role also includes maintaining organized proposal documentation, templates, version control, meeting notes, knowledge repositories, and internal process documentation to ensure consistency across multiple proposal efforts. The Fellow/Intern will continuously identify opportunities to improve proposal quality, increase organizational efficiency, and strengthen future government submissions through lessons learned and process improvements.

## Required Qualifications

The successful candidate demonstrates excellent written and verbal communication skills and is capable of producing professional, accurate, and well-structured documentation suitable for government agencies, research institutions, industry partners, and executive leadership. They possess strong attention to detail, critical thinking, organizational skills, and the ability to manage multiple assignments while maintaining a high standard of quality.

The Fellow/Intern must be comfortable working in a fully remote environment and demonstrate professionalism, accountability, initiative, adaptability, and a willingness to continuously learn new concepts related to government contracting, grants, proposal development, artificial intelligence, and business strategy.

The candidate should have practical experience using AI harnesses such as Claude Code or equivalent AI-powered coding and research environments to improve research, document development, proposal writing, workflow automation, and technical analysis. All AI-generated content must be reviewed, verified, and refined before being used in official organizational documents.

The Fellow/Intern should possess working knowledge of modern Artificial Intelligence technologies and demonstrate literacy in concepts including BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers), YOLO (You Only Look Once), Large Language Models (LLMs), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Prompt Engineering. The individual should understand how these technologies support research, proposal development, automation, and strategic decision-making.

The successful candidate demonstrates strong document writing abilities and can prepare reports, summaries, meeting notes, research papers, proposal sections, executive briefings, and other professional documentation while following organizational writing standards.

## Working Standards

The Fellow/Intern is expected to follow the organization's Agile culture and actively participate in sprint planning, daily stand-up meetings, backlog refinement, sprint reviews, retrospectives, and continuous improvement activities. All assigned work should be planned, tracked, documented, and completed according to Agile principles while maintaining transparency with leadership and team members.

The individual must comply with all organizational cybersecurity policies and best practices when handling confidential information, proposal documentation, customer information, research data, organizational knowledge, and AI-generated content. This includes protecting sensitive information, using approved software and systems, maintaining secure credentials, following access control procedures, and ensuring that all project materials remain confidential.

The Fellow/Intern is expected to maintain organized documentation, consistent file structures, version control practices, and detailed records of meetings, communications, proposal activities, and strategic initiatives to support organizational knowledge management and long-term operational success.

The role requires a commitment to continuous learning, professional development, collaboration, ethical decision-making, and maintaining the highest standards of integrity while representing the organization internally and externally.

## Core Skills

The ideal candidate demonstrates strong capabilities in government contracting research, grant proposal development, technical writing, strategic planning, business analysis, project coordination, stakeholder communication, relationship management, documentation, and organizational planning.

The Fellow/Intern should be proficient in using AI harnesses to support research, document generation, coding assistance, workflow automation, and strategic analysis while understanding the importance of validating AI-generated content for accuracy, compliance, and quality.

The successful candidate possesses excellent analytical thinking, problem-solving, time management, communication, collaboration, leadership, adaptability, and organizational skills. They are capable of working independently in a remote environment while effectively collaborating with cross-functional teams, external partners, Principal Investigators, Key Personnel, and executive leadership to achieve organizational objectives.

## Expected Outcomes

By successfully performing this role, the Fellow/Intern will contribute to the organization's ability to identify new government funding opportunities, establish strategic partnerships through Teaming Agreements, secure Letters of Commitment from Principal Investigators and Key Personnel, produce high-quality ORBITAL Snapshots and proposal documentation, strengthen capture strategies, improve proposal competitiveness, and support the successful submission of government grants and contracts. The Fellow/Intern will also contribute to continuous process improvement, organizational knowledge management, and the responsible use of AI technologies to increase efficiency and proposal quality while maintaining compliance with organizational standards and government requirements.
