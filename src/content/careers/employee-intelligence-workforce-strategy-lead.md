## Role level

Senior-level.

## Opportunity

This is a paid Trustora opportunity. Compensation and other employment terms are discussed with shortlisted candidates and depend on the scope of the role, location, and applicable requirements.

## Position Overview

The Senior Employee Intelligence & Workforce Strategy Lead shapes how Trustora understands difficult work and the people, employment model, and working conditions needed to do it well. You will help clients and internal teams move from a broad hiring or workforce question to a grounded plan for capability, role design, country context, employee support, and accountable execution.

This is a senior individual-contributor and leadership role. It combines strategic framing with enough operating discipline to make the recommendation usable: the question, evidence, assumptions, decision owner, and next step should remain visible after the conversation ends.

## Responsibilities

- Lead discovery and workforce-strategy engagements for specialist technical, scientific, software, and mission-led teams.
- Translate a client’s technical or organizational problem into capability requirements, role scope, team interfaces, and a practical employment or talent path.
- Connect employee-intelligence work to Trustora’s EoR, people-operations, workplace, mobility, and employee-experience capabilities without blurring ownership or making legal claims beyond the available facts.
- Develop clear workforce maps, decision briefs, role architectures, operating plans, and executive recommendations grounded in evidence and explicit assumptions.
- Guide senior client conversations and internal reviews, including trade-offs around location, work model, access, support, sequencing, and escalation.
- Coach colleagues on questioning, documentation, specialist-talent context, and the difference between a useful recommendation and an unsupported promise.
- Identify repeatable patterns in specialist employment and turn them into better methods, content, and service handoffs.

## Required Qualifications

- Significant experience in workforce strategy, specialist talent, people operations, organizational consulting, employee experience, or an adjacent field with comparable responsibility.
- A demonstrated ability to advise on complex work where technical or scientific context changes the people and operating decisions.
- Strong synthesis and writing skills: you can move from interviews, documents, and ambiguous signals to a concise, reviewable point of view.
- Experience leading senior stakeholders through decisions, trade-offs, and next steps in a distributed or cross-border environment.
- Judgment about privacy, employment boundaries, country-specific uncertainty, and when to involve qualified legal, tax, immigration, or other professional advisers.
- A collaborative leadership style that raises the quality of the system around the work rather than creating a single point of dependency.

## Working Standards

Trustora’s employee intelligence work is not surveillance or a generic talent database. It is context around a specialist hire: the problem, capability, team, country, workplace, and decisions that need a clear owner. You should be comfortable holding that distinction in client work, writing, and internal practice.

## Expected Outcomes

Clients leave with a clearer view of the capability they need, the employment and workforce questions that matter, and the next decision they can responsibly make. Trustora’s teams gain reusable methods and a stronger connection between specialist talent insight and the employee experience that follows.
