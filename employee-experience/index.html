Employee intelligence

Context about the work. Never surveillance of the person.

Our view is that employee intelligence means understanding the conditions around specialist work: the capability, the role, the team, the country, the workplace, and the decisions that need a clear owner.

It should not mean keystroke monitoring, demographic inference, or presence metrics disguised as contribution.
